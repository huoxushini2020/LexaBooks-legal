Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026 · Lexa Books (iOS)

English

Lexa Books (“the App”) is provided by YU LIU. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

The App is designed to work on your device. We do not require an account to read books, and we do not operate a Lexa cloud service that collects your reading content for the App Store version.

  • Documents you open (EPUB, PDF, TXT) are processed locally on your device and stored in the App’s sandbox or locations you choose (such as Files).
  • Reading position / recent books are saved locally on your device to resume reading.
  • Optional system features you trigger (system Translate, Speak / TTS, Share, Copy) are provided by Apple or the destination you choose. Their privacy practices apply when you use those features.

Analytics & tracking

The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking for advertising. We do not sell your personal data.

Network access

Network access may be used only when you explicitly use a feature that needs it (for example, sharing to another service). Core reading of local files does not require sending your book content to Lexa servers.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit children’s personal information through the App.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Effective date” above will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at GitHub Issues or email via the contact method listed on the Support page.

中文 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日 · Lexa Books（iOS）

Lexa Books（「本应用」）由 YU LIU 提供。本政策说明本应用如何处理信息。

我们收集哪些信息

本应用以在设备本地运行为主。阅读书籍不需要注册账号；App Store 版本也不会把您的书籍内容上传到 Lexa 自有云服务。

  • 您打开的文档（EPUB / PDF / TXT）在设备本地处理，并保存在应用沙盒或您选择的位置（如「文件」）。
  • 阅读进度 / 最近阅读仅保存在本机，用于继续阅读。
  • 您主动使用的系统功能（系统翻译、朗读、共享、拷贝）由苹果系统或您选择的目标应用提供，适用对方的隐私政策。

分析与追踪

本应用不包含第三方广告 SDK，也不为广告目的进行跨应用追踪。我们不出售您的个人数据。

网络访问

仅在您明确使用需要联网的功能时（例如共享到其他服务）才会访问网络。本地文件阅读本身不需要向 Lexa 服务器发送书籍内容。

联系我们

隐私相关问题请通过 GitHub Issues 或支持页公布的方式联系。

  • ユーザーが明示的に使うシステム機能（システムの翻訳、読み上げ、共有、コピー）は Apple または共有先のサービスが提供し、それぞれのプライバシー方針が適用されます。

本アプリに第三者広告 SDK は含まれず、広告目的のクロスアプリトラッキングも行いません。個人データを販売しません。
